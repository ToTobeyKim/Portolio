Epic Games Library

Applications: Figma, Photoshop

Self-initiated concept project for portfolio purposes. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games.

Redesigning the Epic Games desktop launcher to reduce friction in everyday actions: launching a game, browsing a library, discovering something new.

Challenge

Despite Epic's status as a major industry player, its desktop launcher suffers from poor visual hierarchy, making it difficult for users to quickly identify important actions or content. Key journeys like launching or discovering games require more clicks than necessary.

The scope narrowed from "redesign every page" to focusing on the Library and side navigation, the areas where usability issues most directly affected how players accessed and managed their games.

Research

Six participants, ages 16 to 30, compared their experience across Epic Games, Steam, and the EA App. Most found Epic's library unintuitive and visually lacking, especially the absence of accessible information like playtime, achievements, or update details. Steam was consistently praised for its clear hierarchy and ease of navigation.

Design

To stay authentic to Epic's identity, the existing typography, color system, and iconography were preserved. The redesign focused on refining and clarifying, not reinventing.

A Community tab was added to the sidebar for easier access to social features. The Search Store option was removed from the library view to reduce clutter and confusion between owned and discoverable titles.

The first prototype, built directly from the wireframe, felt overly simplistic and lacked visual depth. Contextual information was added to the top of the page in a separate container, creating a clearer distinction between interactive and informational content.

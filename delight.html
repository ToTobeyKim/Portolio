Delight

Applications: Illustrator, Photoshop

A home improvement brand built around people and connection, helping individuals restore their homes, and by extension, their lives.

Brand

Delight's mission centers on a human-centered approach to home repair, and that guided every decision in the identity. Orange was chosen for its strong association with construction, energy, and repair, a lighter hue softened the tone, removing the sense of caution often linked to darker shades and creating a warm, optimistic feel.

The Archer typeface, a geometric slab serif, reinforces that same balance of structure and warmth: sturdy, architectural forms evoke foundation and reliability, while friendly curves keep the brand approachable.

Detail

A subtle yet defining element of the logo is the italicized lowercase "i" in Delight. Emotive and playful, it sits at the center of the wordmark, symbolizing people and reflecting the company's commitment to keeping humans at the heart of everything it does.

Typeface

Archer was designed by Jonathan Hoefler and Tobias Frere-Jones in 2001 for Martha Stewart Living, a publication dense with lists, recipes, charts, and tables that demanded a typeface that was personable, straightforward, and credible.

Ball terminals, typically reserved for lowercase letterforms, were uncharacteristically applied to capitals as well, producing a typeface that's friendly without being silly, and attractive without being flashy.

Intent Collective

Role  Graphic Designer Timeline  4 Weeks Team  Intent Collective

A design collective founded on the belief that design itself is the manifestation of intent — every detail, mark, and gesture must be deliberate.

Statement

Design is the manifestation of intent. Every detail, every mark, gesture, and experience must be deliberate. At Intent, design is never an accident — it is an act of intent.

Intent bridges the rigor of strategy with the poetry of visual storytelling, specializing in Brand Experience and People Experience through thoughtful process, strategic clarity, and message-driven design.

Identity

The logo was created to represent a forward-thinking design team — a mark that felt simple, modern, and strong. Gotham, a geometric sans serif, was chosen for its clean lines and contemporary character, aligning with the company's focus on clarity and purpose.

The tittle of the "i" in Intent was enlarged and shaded red, symbolizing the sun, a target, and a point of focus — a visual metaphor for direction and presence, as in "I am here." This small, distinctive detail keeps the logo recognizable even from a distance.

An alternate color scheme was developed for dark backgrounds: the wordmark in white, enclosed within a red square, maintaining the same precision and presence across applications.

Typeface

Gotham was designed by Tobias Frere-Jones in 2000, inspired by the bold capital letters found on lithographed posters, enamel signs, and commercial facades across early twentieth-century New York City.

It first appeared in GQ magazine in 2001 and gained international attention in 2007 after being adopted by the Obama presidential campaign — today it sits in the permanent collection of MoMA.

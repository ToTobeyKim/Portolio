Doordash

Role  UX/UI Designer Timeline  3 Days Team  Moonrock

A concept marketplace built directly within Discord, connecting community engagement with ecommerce functionality.

Brief

The project envisioned a next-generation online marketplace for a Fortune 500 company, modeled after DoorDash's visual system and material design guidelines. As a client-directed contract, the priority was a clean, modern site that adhered strictly to the established color palette and proprietary iconography.

Audience

The target demographic was Discord users aged 25–34. Aligning the visual language with Discord's modern, minimal aesthetic — while drawing on DoorDash's approachable, dynamic identity — let the product resonate with its core audience without feeling out of place on either platform.

Design

With a compressed three-day timeline, ideation and mockups were expedited. The user journey was built so most pages could be reached within two clicks, with repetitive sections streamlined to reduce cognitive load.

The wireframe used a 12-column grid with 60px margins and 20px gutters, DoorDash's standard layout convention, with particular attention paid to product reviews and category selection.

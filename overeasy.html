OverEasy

Applications: Figma, Illustrator, InDesign

A recipe app that reimagines cooking around the ingredients already in your kitchen, helping reduce food waste through an AI-powered Fridge feature.

Problem

Cooking content has exploded across social media, making traditional recipe apps feel stagnant against free, engaging alternatives. OverEasy bridges that gap with a dynamic, personalized experience built around what's already in the user's kitchen, rather than static recipe lists.

Brand

The name draws from "over easy," the egg-cooking style that evokes simplicity, comfort, and familiarity. Red, yellow, and green were selected for their appetite-stimulating associations, with yellow as the dominant color reinforcing both the egg reference and an optimistic, approachable tone.

The minimalist egg-shaped mark uses as few lines as possible, subtly inspired by Gudetama's relaxed, relatable personality.

Research

A competitive audit against Paprika, Big Oven, and Yummly found pantry tracking and waste-reducing recipe suggestions were largely absent or shallow across the category.

Interviews with 8 participants across age groups found most no longer use dedicated cooking apps, citing lack of personalization and unnecessary complexity, pointing OverEasy toward simplicity and relevance.

Design

The five core screens, Home, Fridge, Search, Saved, Settings, were structured around a clear hierarchy between primary and secondary tabs, spotlighting the features that set OverEasy apart from competitors.

Early prototype testing surfaced poor color contrast, inconsistent grouping, and too many steps to complete common tasks. The palette was refined for accessibility, navigation was streamlined, and interactive elements were redesigned for clarity.

System

A full brand guideline was developed to extend the identity across the application.

About

I believe the most important part of graphic design is its connection to humanity. Understanding how people think, feel, and interact with the world is what drives me as a designer. With a background in Psychology and a minor in Computer Science, I focus on the intersection of human behavior and technology to create solutions that are both functional and empathetic.

My experience combines design, programming, and leadership, giving me the technical and people centered skills to build intuitive products and systems. I have managed teams, organized events, and led projects that required clear communication, coordination, and problem solving. I have also conducted user focused research to better understand how individuals engage with products and digital experiences.

At the core, I am passionate about designing user friendly experiences that bridge the gap between technology and human needs while keeping empathy at the center of design.
